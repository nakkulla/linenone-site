ASHG 2026 poster · Montréal

Biliary microbiome stratifies pancreaticobiliary cancer prognosis via unsupervised clustering and machine learning

Ilsun Yun, Hyemin Kim, Dong Kee Jang, Joo Kyung Park, Semin Lee

Poster board
1006T
Session
Thu, Oct 22, 2026
Time
16:15–18:15

Bile from 77 patients with pancreatic or biliary tract cancer was clustered on genus composition without using survival data. The higher-risk cluster had shorter overall survival (HR 4.04, 95% CI 2.01–8.12). Rerunning the same rule on the 57 biliary tract cancers alone gave HR 5.27 (95% CI 2.25–12.31); those 57 are part of the 77.

One cohort at one centre, with no external validation yet. Microbial function is predicted from 16S data.
